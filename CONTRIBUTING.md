# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep the E Gurl Community C++23, Qt 6, and QML roadmap accurate and useful.

## Useful contributions

- Fixing incorrect explanations, spelling, or broken links.
- Replacing outdated Qt 5 or qmake instructions with documented Qt 6 and CMake practices.
- Adding high-quality free resources from primary or well-established sources.
- Improving platform-neutral instructions for Linux, Windows, or macOS.
- Correcting or clarifying C++, Qt, QML, CMake, testing, or deployment material.
- Improving accessibility or Mermaid diagram readability.

## Resource requirements

Before proposing a resource, check that it:

- Is directly relevant to modern C++, Qt 6, QML, Qt Quick, or the roadmap's engineering tools.
- Is free to access, apart from an optional account when unavoidable.
- Clearly identifies its target version or remains valid independent of version.
- Does not teach Qt 5/qmake setup as the recommended approach for a new Qt 6 project.
- Adds something the existing official documentation or courses do not already cover better.
- Comes from an official source, recognized educator, conference, or experienced Qt/C++ organization.

## Pull-request checklist

- [ ] The change is focused and explained clearly.
- [ ] Every new link opens successfully.
- [ ] Version-sensitive claims link to an official source.
- [ ] Instructions remain editor- and distribution-neutral unless a section is explicitly platform-specific.
- [ ] Mermaid blocks have been previewed successfully.
- [ ] The table of contents and heading structure remain readable.

Small fixes are welcome. For a large restructuring, open an issue first so the direction can be discussed before extensive work begins.
